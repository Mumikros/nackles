# Support Nackles

Nackles is free and MIT-licensed. Stars, reproducible bug reports, translations and contributions help the project grow.

Financial support is not enabled yet. A verified payment link will be added here after the maintainer completes the payment platform's onboarding. Do not send payments to accounts mentioned in unsolicited issues or comments.

## Maintainer setup

GitHub Sponsors supports maintainers residing in Turkey. Complete the official onboarding at https://github.com/sponsors/accounts, including the required identity, payment and tax details. Those details belong on the payment provider's secure site, never in this repository.

Once the profile is approved, uncomment `github: [Mumikros]` in `.github/FUNDING.yml` and add the verified public Sponsors profile link here. Optional external platforms should be added only after ownership and payout eligibility have been confirmed.

Suggested optional tiers: a small one-time thank-you, a monthly supporter tier and a higher monthly contributor-support tier. Avoid promising response times or features that cannot be sustained. Donations do not change the MIT license.

References: [GitHub Sponsors regions](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors), [personal account setup](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-personal-account).
